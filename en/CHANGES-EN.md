# Changes — English Edition

## v2.3.1-EN

- English-language edition of Sentinel Blacktrace Command v2.3.1.
- All operator-facing UI, logs, phase briefs, summary labels and override text
  are English. The artist signature `原罪知鸮` is the only intentional Chinese
  mark and is always visible in the lower-right corner.
- Eight music tracks and rotating MFPlay playback.
- English `quotes-en.txt` ghost-quote corpus; external `QuotesFile` remains
  configurable.
- `IdleMinutes` in `config.ini` is applied by `install.bat` as the Windows
  screen-saver idle timeout. `0` means double-click only.
- Version resource: 2.3.1.0, OriginalFilename `Sentinel-v2.3.1-EN.exe`.

The Chinese v2.3.1 edition remains available separately.
