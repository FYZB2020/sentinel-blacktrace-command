# Music — Chinese edition

The release zip contains the eight tracks. The repository does not commit the MP3s
to keep the repo small and to avoid redistributing audio whose rights may belong to
someone else.

To build from source:

1. Put your own `.mp3` or `.wav` files in this folder.
2. Keep the folder name `music` (or set `MusicDir` in `config.ini`).
3. Rebuild; the program discovers the files at runtime.

Tracks shipped in the release:

1. A sudden blow.mp3
2. A sudden blow (v2).mp3
3. The wheels of the world.mp3
4. The wheels of the world 2.59.mp3
5. The ghost in the machine v2.mp3
6. A wilderness of mirrors.mp3
7. Slouching towards Bethlehem.mp3
8. Slouching towards Bethlehem V2.mp3

`F8`, `M`, and the hidden bottom-right click switch tracks. `Music=off` disables
the music layer without disabling sound effects.
